# Contributing to the Project Journal

Applies to people and AI tools alike.

## General rules
Every entry is accurate, verifiable, concise, contextualized, consistent with `reference/domain/glossary.md`, understandable months later. Cite the source (minutes, document, decision) whenever possible.

## Weekly (entry point of new information)
A shared snapshot: main activities, decisions, issues, risks, significant observations, plans for next week. Not a copy of the minutes.

## Monthly
A higher reading: recurring patterns, consolidated learnings, value stream trend, observed impact of the agent-supervised model, business case signals, systemic risks, organizational implications. Do not repeat the weeklies: synthesize them.

## Optional registers
- **Decision record**: decisions with project impact, chosen among alternatives, or reversing a previous one. Not for operational details.
- **Learning**: only with recurring or team-validated evidence; a single episode is an observation.
- **Risk**: new risk, changed likelihood/impact, new mitigation, closure.
- **Metrics**: verifiable data only, with source and period.

## Style
Plain, objective language; short sentences; facts before interpretations; no opinions, no promotional tone, no duplication. Link entries (weekly → decision, decision → learning, learning → metric).

## AI
AI may propose weeklies, decision records, learnings, risk and metric updates and links. Proposals are validated by the team before being consolidated. Responsibility stays with people.
